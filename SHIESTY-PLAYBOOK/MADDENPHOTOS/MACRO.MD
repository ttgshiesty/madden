## DEFENSE MACRO ##
***Top Macro Setups***
1. The Glitch Edge/Contain Press (Cover 3/Cover 6 Base)
-- Show Blitz: Linebackers
-- Defensive Line: Pinch, then slant/crash out
-- QB Contain: Both sides
-- Result: Forces offensive tackles wide and lets speed rushers (like Micah Parsons) flash untouched around the edge instantly.

2. Fast Man Coverage Lockdown Macro
-- Show Blitz: Linebackers
-- Coverage Shading: Inside
-- Route Commit: Inside
-- QB Contain: Both
-- D-Line Technique: Pinch
-- SAM & WILL Linebackers: Set to Curl-Flat
-- Result: Ties up quick inside slants and tight end crossers while maintaining a 6-man swarm over the middle.

3. Cover 4 Quarters / Mabel Zone Macro
-- Defensive Line: Pinch and crash down
-- Outer Cornerbacks/Safeties: Hard flats or 30-yard cloud flats depending on hash assignment
-- User Linebacker: Controlled over the middle seam/hook zone
-- Result: Blankets deep sideline corner routes while clamping down underneath constraints.
